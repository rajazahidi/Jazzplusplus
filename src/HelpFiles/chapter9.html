Chapter 9: FAQ & Troubleshooting

Why do I hear no sound during playback?

If notes are playing in the timeline but no sound is produced, check the following:

  1. MIDI Output Device: Open Settings → MIDI Settings and verify that a valid output synthesizer is selected.
    • On Windows, select Microsoft GS Wavetable Synth or an active virtual synth like FluidSynth.
    • On Linux, ensure an ALSA synth (e.g. fluidsynth or timidity) is running and selected.
    • On macOS, select the Apple DLS Synth or CoreMIDI output.
  2. Volume Levels: Ensure the track volume (CC7) in the Track Window or Mixer is not set to 0, and that the track is not muted.
  3. Windows Volume Mixer: Check your system volume tray icon to verify that the Jazz++ application output is unmuted and set to an audible level.

How can I eliminate audio latency or stuttering?

Audio latency refers to the delay between striking a note and hearing the sound:

  • Open Settings → Audio Settings.
  • Reduce the Audio Buffer Size to 256 or 128 samples for immediate response during live recording.
  • If you hear crackling, clicks, or dropouts, your CPU cannot fill the small buffer in time. Increase the buffer size to 512 or 1024 samples.

Why does Windows Defender / SmartScreen show a blue warning?

When downloading or installing Jazz++ on Windows, you may encounter a prompt stating "Windows protected your PC - Microsoft Defender SmartScreen prevented an unrecognized app from starting":

  • Cause: Microsoft SmartScreen flags newly released open-source software that does not possess an expensive commercial EV Code Signing certificate.
  • Safety: Jazz++ is completely safe, 100% open-source, and contains zero adware, spyware, or trackers. All build steps and source code are publicly verifiable on GitHub.
  • How to Run: Click "More info" on the SmartScreen dialog, and then click the "Run anyway" button. The installer or application will launch normally.

How do I use SoundFonts (.sf2) for realistic sounds?

For enhanced sound realism beyond the basic General MIDI synthesizer:

  • Download any high-quality General MIDI SoundFont (such as GeneralUser GS or FluidR3_GM).
  • On Windows, use a virtual synth driver such as VirtualMIDISynth, which loads SoundFonts into memory and creates a virtual MIDI device selectable in Jazz++.
  • On Linux, run FluidSynth with your soundfont: fluidsynth -a alsa -m alsa_seq /path/to/soundfont.sf2 and select it in Jazz++ MIDI settings.

What should I do if a note gets stuck (hangs)?

Occasionally, an external MIDI cable disconnect or sudden sequence stop can cause a synthesizer note to stay sustained. Press Esc or click the Panic button on the toolbar to immediately silence all notes across all 16 channels.
