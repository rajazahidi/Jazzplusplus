Chapter 4: Track Window & Sequencer

The Track Window is the primary command center of Jazz++. It displays the multi-track arrangement of your song, timeline measures, track controls, and project navigation.

Track Parameters & Controls

Each track row in the Track Window contains essential controls:

  • Track Number: Numerical index of the track. Track 1 is always the Master Track.
  • Mute (M): Silences the track during playback.
  • Solo (S): Solos the track, silencing all other un-soloed tracks.
  • Track Name: Descriptive name of the instrument or voice. Double-click to rename.
  • MIDI Channel: Destination MIDI channel (1 through 16). Channel 10 is reserved for standard General MIDI percussion.
  • Program Voice: The instrument patch assigned to this track. Click to choose from General MIDI (GM), Roland GS, or Yamaha XG voice banks.
  • Volume & Pan: Default channel volume (CC7) and stereo panning (CC10).

Ruler, Meter & Speed Controls

  • Timeline Ruler: Displays bar numbers (1, 5, 9, 13...). Click anywhere in the ruler to jump the playback cursor to that measure.
  • Speed (BPM): Shows current song tempo. Double-click to type a precise tempo (e.g. 128.5 BPM), or click and drag left/right to adjust smoothly.
  • Meter Change: Change time signature at any measure (e.g. 4/4, 3/4, 6/8, 7/8). Select Misc → Meter Change to add time signature markers.

Track Operations & Editing

  • Select Range: Click and drag across measure blocks in the track grid to select a region.
  • Cut / Copy / Paste / Replicate: Standard clipboard operations to duplicate riffs and choruses across tracks and measures.
  • Quantize Dialog: Aligns note start times to rhythmic grid subdivisions (1/4, 1/8, 1/16, 1/32, or triplets) with adjustable swing percentage and humanize jitter.
  • Transpose Dialog: Shifts selected notes up or down chromatically by semitones, or within the musical key.
  • Velocity Scaling: Adjusts dynamics by compressing, expanding, or adding a constant offset to velocity values.
  • Event Filter Dialog: Selectively filters, deletes, or modifies specific event types (e.g. remove all pitch bend data, or isolate notes within a velocity range).
  • Cleanup: Removes duplicate overlapping notes and zero-length events automatically.

The Mixer Console

Press Ctrl+M to open the Mixer window. The mixer presents a studio console layout with channel faders, stereo pan pots, mute/solo buttons, and real-time LED volume meters for all active MIDI and audio tracks.
