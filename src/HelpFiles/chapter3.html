Chapter 3: Quick Start & Workflow Overview

This chapter walks you through your first music production session in Jazz++ v6.0.0, from creating a blank project to recording melodies and exporting audio.

Creating Your First Project

  1. Launch Jazz++. A blank song with 16 tracks is automatically initialized.
  2. Notice that Track 1 is reserved as the Master Track. The master track handles global tempo (BPM), time signature (meter changes), and song structure markers. Always record your musical instruments on Track 2 and above.
  3. Click on the track name of Track 2 to open the Track Settings Dialog.
    • Enter a descriptive name (e.g. Acoustic Piano).
    • Set the MIDI Channel to 1.
    • Select an instrument from the General MIDI list (e.g. 0: Acoustic Grand Piano).

Recording and Playback

Jazz++ provides flexible methods to compose music — using a live MIDI keyboard, the computer mouse in the Piano Roll, or the automated Virtual Session Band:

  • Metronome: Click the Metronome button on the toolbar or press M. The metronome provides an audible count-in click during playback and recording.
  • Tempo Adjustment: Click the speed: 120 box in the ruler to change the tempo in Beats Per Minute (BPM), or click and drag horizontally to slide the tempo smoothly.
  • Live Recording: Select Track 2, position the playback cursor at Bar 1, and click the red Record button (or press R). Play notes on your connected MIDI keyboard. Press Spacebar to stop recording.
  • Playback: Press the Spacebar or click the green Play button to play back your performance. Use the Play Loop button to loop a designated range of measures continuously.

Editing in the Piano Roll

Double-click any recorded track bar or click the Piano Window button on the toolbar to open the graphical Piano Roll:

  • The vertical piano keyboard on the left represents pitch (notes from C-1 to G9).
  • The horizontal grid represents time in measures, beats, and subdivisions.
  • Use the left mouse button to draw new notes or drag existing notes to change their pitch and timing.
  • Drag the right edge of any note to extend or shorten its duration.
  • Use the velocity lane at the bottom to adjust individual note volume and dynamics.

Using the Virtual Session Band

If you don't have a full rhythm section recorded, Jazz++ can generate one for you instantly!

  1. Open the Virtual Session Band from the Tools menu.
  2. Choose a musical style (e.g. Jazz Swing, Bossa Nova, or Blues Shuffle).
  3. Select your chord progression or click Generate Accompaniment.
  4. The engine generates synchronized RealTracks (Walking Bass, Piano Comping) and RealDrums directly into your project!

Saving & Exporting

  • Save Project: Click File → Save (Ctrl+S) to save your project in the native Jazz++ .jaz format, preserving all audio tracks, mixer settings, and DSP effects.
  • Export Standard MIDI: Select File → Export MIDI to create a universal .mid file compatible with any DAW, notation program, or hardware keyboard.
  • Export Audio WAV: Select File → Export Audio (WAV) to render your entire song as an uncompressed 16-bit or 24-bit stereo audio file.
