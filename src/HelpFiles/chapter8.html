Standard MIDI Continuous Controllers (CC)

CC NumberStandard NameFunction
CC 0 / 32Bank Select (MSB / LSB)Selects sound bank for instruments beyond 128 GM presets
CC 1Modulation WheelVibrato and modulation depth
CC 7Channel VolumePrimary channel volume level (0 = silent, 127 = maximum)
CC 10PanStereo panning position (0 = hard left, 64 = center, 127 = hard right)
CC 11ExpressionSecondary volume dynamics for realistic instrumental swells
CC 64Damper / Sustain PedalSustain pedal switch (0–63 = pedal off, 64–127 = pedal depressed)
CC 91Reverb Send LevelAmount of signal sent to external/synthesizer reverb
CC 93Chorus Send LevelAmount of signal sent to external/synthesizer chorus
CC 120 / 123All Sound Off / All Notes OffEmergency silence command silencing all playing notes
