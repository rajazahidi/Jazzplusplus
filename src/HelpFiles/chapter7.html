Chapter 7: Virtual Session Band & Advanced Tools

Jazz++ v6.0.0 introduces breakthrough automated composition and sound synthesis tools designed to accelerate your creative workflow.

Virtual Session Band (RealTracks & RealDrums)

The Virtual Session Band acts as your personal multi-instrument backing group. Whether you are practicing an instrument, sketching song ideas, or producing complete backing tracks, the engine dynamically generates authentic accompaniment tailored to your chords and tempo.

Musical Genres Supported:

  • Jazz Swing: Authentic walking acoustic bass, syncopated Freddie Green-style chord comping on piano/guitar, and brushed swing drums with dynamic hi-hat pedal chicks.
  • Bossa Nova: Gentle nylon-string guitar rhythms, warm syncopated bass, and authentic Brazilian percussion (shaker, surdo, cross-stick snare).
  • Bebop: Fast-tempo, intricate walking basslines and chromatic piano voicing suitable for uptempo improvisation.
  • Funk Groove: Slap and fingerstyle electric bass grooves, tight rhythmic 16th-note clavinet/guitar comping, and punchy funk drums.
  • Smooth Ballad: Gentle arpeggiated piano, sustained acoustic fretless bass, and soft ride cymbal patterns.
  • Blues Shuffle: Driving 12-bar blues shuffle bass, blues-box guitar rhythm, and swinging backbeat drums.
  • Latin Salsa & Reggae: Tumbao basslines, syncopated piano montunos, one-drop reggae drums, and off-beat skank chords.
  • Hard Rock & Metal: Driving eighth-note picked bass, power chord rhythm guitars, and powerful acoustic rock drums.

Procedural Sound Generator (Drums & FX)

Accessed via Tools → Sound Generator, this module creates synthesized percussion instruments and audio effects from mathematical algorithms without requiring external sample libraries:

  • Drum Synthesis:
    • Punchy Bass Drum: Pitch-swept sine oscillator with transient click and exponential amplitude decay.
    • Snare Drum: Dual-component synthesis combining a tuned tone body with white-noise snare rattle.
    • Hi-Hats & Cymbals: Multi-oscillator metallic frequency cluster with bandpass filtering.
    • Hand Claps & Toms: Multi-tap transient bursts with resonant acoustic pitch decays.
  • Sound FX Generator:
    • White Noise Riser / Sweep: Dramatic frequency sweeps for dance music transitions.
    • Sub-Bass Drop: Deep 808-style downward bass drops.
    • 8-Bit Laser & Alien Zap: Retro video game sound effects and sci-fi modular synthesizer patches.
  • Export Options: Sounds can be exported directly to audio tracks or assigned to MIDI note triggers.

Interactive Guitar Fretboard & Chord Explorer

Open Window → Guitar Fretboard to access a visual representation of a stringed instrument neck:

  • Multi-Tuning Support: Standard (E-A-D-G-B-E), Drop D, DADGAD, Open G, Open D, and 7-String guitar tunings.
  • Real-Time Visualizer: Shows note positions in real-time as MIDI plays back.
  • Chord Voicing Library: Click any chord symbol (e.g. Cmaj7, Dm9, G13b9) to view finger placements, intervals, and inversions on the fretboard. Click "Insert into Track" to drop the voiced chord directly into your active MIDI track!

Auto-Accompaniment & Harmony Browser

The Harmony Browser analyzes musical keys and suggests matching scales and progressions:

  • Supports Major, Natural Minor, Harmonic Minor, Melodic Minor, Dorian, Phrygian, Lydian, Mixolydian, Locrian, Blues, and Bebop scales.
  • Generates synchronized arpeggios, bass runs, and harmonized melodic lines matching any selected musical scale.
