Chapter 6: Audio Subsystem & DSP Effects

Jazz++ v6.0.0 incorporates a modern 64-bit digital audio subsystem. In addition to MIDI sequencing, you can record vocal and acoustic audio tracks, trigger digital audio samples, and process your mix through an integrated suite of studio DSP effects.

Multi-Track Audio Recording & WAV Samples

Audio tracks in Jazz++ function alongside MIDI tracks with sample-accurate synchronization:

  • Adding an Audio Track: In the Track Window, open the Track Settings dialog and check Audio Track.
  • Audio Sample Sets: You can load .wav audio files and map them to keys or tracks. Jazz++ supports uncompressed WAV files with 16-bit, 24-bit, and 32-bit floating-point precision at sample rates from 44.1 kHz up to 192 kHz.
  • Live Audio Recording: Connect a microphone or instrument to your audio interface. Arm the audio track for recording and press the Record button. The audio waveform is captured directly into your project.
  • Waveform Editing: Zoom into audio tracks to inspect waveforms, slice audio regions, adjust gain, and apply fade-in / fade-out curves.

Audio Settings & Latency Tuning

Open Settings → Audio Settings to configure your sound hardware:

  • Sample Rate: Choose 44100 Hz (CD Quality) or 48000 Hz (Broadcast Standard).
  • Audio Buffer Size: Sets the latency of the audio stream.
    • 128 / 256 samples (~3-6 ms): Recommended for real-time monitoring and recording.
    • 512 / 1024 samples (~12-24 ms): Recommended for large mixing projects with multiple simultaneous audio tracks and heavy DSP processing.
  • Audio Backend: Windows uses ultra-low latency WASAPI or WinMM; Linux utilizes ALSA with automatic PulseAudio/PipeWire bridging; macOS uses Apple CoreAudio.
