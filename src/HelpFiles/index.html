Jazz++ MIDI Sequencer & Audio Workstation

Version 6.0.0 Pro Studio • User Manual & Reference Guide

Welcome to Jazz++ 6.0.0! Jazz++ is an advanced open-source MIDI sequencer and digital audio workstation designed for professional music composition, sequencing, and audio production across Windows, Linux, and macOS.

Version 6.0.0 introduces the flagship Virtual Session Band (RealTracks & RealDrums), 64-bit precision Audio DSP Effects (Reverb, Delay, Chorus, Flanger, EQ, Overdrive), procedural sound synthesis, interactive guitar fretboard explorer, auto-accompaniment, and modern multi-platform audio drivers.
